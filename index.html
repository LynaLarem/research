Fault Tolerance in Medical Cyber-Physical Systems through Runtime Monitoring

An artificial pancreas delivers insulin automatically. If its pump fails, the patient receives no insulin and the glucose level can rise to dangerous values. The system detects this from the signals already present in the loop (continuous glucose monitor and insulin doses) and alerts the user on a phone.

  1. Kalman-filter detector. Compares measured and predicted glucose and analyses the residuals in a sliding window, with a CUSUM score for slow drifts in the adult profile.
  2. LISA-based detector. Combines a glucose fault metric, an insulin fault metric and the glucose slope in a weighted score, with temporal confirmation.
  3. Decision fusion. Three confidence levels: normal, suspected failure, confirmed failure.

Thresholds and window sizes are calibrated offline for each patient profile and evaluated in the SimGlucose simulator (UVa/Padova) on a virtual child, adolescent and adult.
